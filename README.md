One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026: a 1024x1024 poster drawn programmatically with Pillow from that day's reporting, so every letter is crisp. Under the day's full date, the lead is Shenzhen — Trump arriving for the APEC summit ahead of the first Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral — with cards for the US-Iran strikes around the Gulf, Ukraine's strike on the Crimea bridge, the United States losing its measles-elimination status, the magnitude 6.0 earthquake in Papua New Guinea, Israel's Knesset electing a new speaker, and a report pricing everyday chemicals' yearly toll at $1 trillion, plus an also-today strip (ASEAN wraps in Manila, Earthshot Prize winners, a possible Kim meeting). The program at the root copies entry.png to /output and first checks it against the gate's own limits (a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
